Analyses the global diversity and population structure of 23 leading Plasmodium falciparum vaccine candidate antigens across more than 2,600 genomes, using a new R package, VaxPack. Current vaccine formulations are based on rare haplotypes, and most antigens show diversity consistent with balancing selection.
