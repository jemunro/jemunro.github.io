Analyses worldwide sequence diversity and population structure across 23 leading *Plasmodium falciparum* vaccine candidate antigens. Using population-genetic analyses supported by the VaxPack R package, the study identifies extensive antigen variation and evidence of balancing selection relevant to vaccine design.
