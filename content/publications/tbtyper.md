Introduces TBtypeR, an R package and accompanying Nextflow workflow for detecting and classifying mixed-strain *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* complex infections from whole-genome sequencing data. Its phylogenetic SNP approach detects minor strains at frequencies as low as 1% and identifies substantially more mixed infections than conventional methods in a large published dataset.
