Characterises progressive myoclonus epilepsy associated with the *SLC7A6OS* c.191A>G variant across five families. Shared haplotypes point to a founder mutation arising approximately 1,100 years ago, providing insight into the variant's geographic distribution and the clinical spectrum of this rare disorder.
