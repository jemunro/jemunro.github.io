Characterises pDCA, a 37-kb plasmid found in *Xanthobacter* and *Starkeya* isolates from a 1,2-dichloroethane-contaminated site. The plasmid carries dehalogenase genes involved in pollutant breakdown and provides evidence for the mobility of catabolic functions among environmental bacteria.
