Presents PLASTER, a data processing pipeline for accurate allele typing of PacBio SMRT-sequenced amplicons, and uses it to type CYP2D6 alleles in 377 Solomon Islanders. CYP2D6 is hard to type because of frequent copy number variants and paralogous pseudogenes, which the long-read approach is designed to handle.
