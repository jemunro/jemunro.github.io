Introduces PvAmpSeq, an amplicon sequencing assay targeting 11 SNP-rich regions of the Plasmodium vivax genome, and applies it with identity-by-descent and statistical models to classify recurrent infections. It is demonstrated on field samples from a clinical trial in the Solomon Islands and a cohort in Peru.
