Tests a pilot-scale membrane bioreactor for removing 1,2-dichloroethane from groundwater, tracking the microbial community by 16S rDNA pyrosequencing and a new dhlA qPCR assay. Degradation was sustained after a 26-day adaptation, and dhlA abundance tracked the amount of DCA treated.
