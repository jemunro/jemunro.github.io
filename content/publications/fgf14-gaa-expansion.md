Identifies an intronic GAA repeat expansion in *FGF14* as a cause of autosomal-dominant adult-onset cerebellar ataxia. Findings from Australian and German cohorts establish the expansion as a recurrent disease mechanism and link repeat size to penetrance and age at onset.
