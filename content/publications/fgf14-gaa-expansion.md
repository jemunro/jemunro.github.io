Identifies an intronic GAA repeat expansion in FGF14 as a cause of adult-onset cerebellar ataxia, found in 4.2% of an Australian cohort and replicated in a German cohort. Expansions over 335 repeats appear fully penetrant, and repeat length correlates with age at onset.
