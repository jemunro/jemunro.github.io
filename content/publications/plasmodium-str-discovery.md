Develops and validates a genome-wide set of short tandem repeat markers from large collections of *Plasmodium falciparum* and *P. vivax* genome sequences. The study introduces quality-control approaches for STR genotypes and demonstrates their use in analysing parasite population structure and genetic diversity, with results accessible through PlasmoSTR.
