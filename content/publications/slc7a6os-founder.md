Refines the clinical picture of progressive myoclonus epilepsy caused by the SLC7A6OS c.191A>G variant in five families. Shared haplotypes indicate a common ancestor about 1,100 years ago, consistent with migration between the eastern Mediterranean, Iberia and Puerto Rico.
