Develops PvAmpSeq, a targeted sequencing assay covering 11 SNP-rich regions of the *Plasmodium vivax* genome, and combines it with identity-by-descent and statistical classification to investigate recurrent malaria infections. The approach is evaluated using samples from the Solomon Islands and Peru.
