Introduces TBtypeR, which compares whole-genome sequencing data with a phylogenetic SNP panel of over 10,000 sites and 164 phylotypes to classify Mycobacterium tuberculosis mixed-strain infections at frequencies as low as 1%. Applied to a published dataset of 5,000 samples, it identified 305 mixed-strain infections, six-fold more than previously reported.
