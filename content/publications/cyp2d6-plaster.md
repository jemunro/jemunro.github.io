Develops PLASTER, a pipeline for high-resolution allele typing from PacBio long-read amplicon sequencing, and applies it to the pharmacogenetically important *CYP2D6* locus in 377 Solomon Islanders. The approach resolves complex haplotypes and structural variation that are difficult to characterise with short reads.
