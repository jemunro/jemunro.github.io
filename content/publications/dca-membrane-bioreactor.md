Investigates the adaptation of a pilot-scale membrane bioreactor for removing 1,2-dichloroethane from groundwater. Sequencing of 16S rRNA genes and quantitative PCR of the *dhlA* degradation gene track microbial community changes and biodegradation activity during reactor operation.
